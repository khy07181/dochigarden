Descriptions

Tagline

A quiet, native focus timer for your Mac's menu bar.

Short

Cloister is a calm, native focus timer that lives in your Mac's menu bar. Start a session, let the golden ring close, and watch your focused hours stack up — kept on your device, with no account and no cloud.

Long

Cloister is a focus timer for macOS, made for people who want to concentrate and keep a record of it — without the noise. Most timers are either a bare stopwatch or a tracker buried under tags, projects, and to-dos. Cloister sits in between: start a focus session from the menu bar, take the break as a real part of the cycle, and each completed round stacks up into streaks, a year-long heatmap, and a quiet ledger of your focused hours. Everything stays on your Mac — no account, no cloud, no analytics. It's a small, deliberate app built to be used for a long time: native SwiftUI, notarized, and self-distributed with automatic updates.

Key features

  • Menu-bar focus timer with a golden progress ring — start, pause, and break without leaving your work
  • Focus + Break as one intentional cycle — a completed round is a "Stack"
  • Streaks, daily goals, and a full-year focus heatmap
  • Insights: focus trends, weekly comparison, lifetime totals
  • 100% local — stored on your Mac, no account, no cloud, no trackers
  • Native macOS (SwiftUI), light & dark, keyboard shortcuts
  • Optional iCloud sync across your own Macs (Pro)
  • Notarized, installable via Homebrew or DMG, with automatic updates

In the maker's words

Cloister came from wanting a focus timer that was neither too simple nor too complex — one that treats rest as part of the cycle and quietly records the work, so that consistency becomes something you can see.
